# LunaCore v0.2 — Context, Purpose & Adaptive Support

## Reasoning Flow

Faith / Values
→ Context & History
→ Purpose
→ Risk & Uncertainty
→ Support Level
→ Human Choice
→ Outcome / Learning

## Context

History may inform present support, but it must not define the person.

Historical information is weighted by:

- relevance
- confidence

Old or weak evidence should exert less influence than current, relevant information.

## Purpose

LunaCore should understand what the human is trying to accomplish.

Purpose is evaluated using:

- stated purpose
- clarity
- alignment

Purpose helps shape support but cannot override serious safety concerns.

## Adaptive Support

The existing v0.1 Active Support engine remains authoritative for risk level.

Context and purpose enrich the decision rather than replacing risk reasoning.

Core constraints:

- Purpose cannot override safety.
- Safety should not erase purpose unnecessarily.
- History informs without becoming destiny.
- Strong intervention should decrease when circumstances improve.
- Human agency remains central.

## Future Learning Layer

A later version may record outcomes from prior support decisions.

That learning system must avoid:

- permanent negative labeling
- treating one event as identity
- silently increasing control
- feedback loops that reinforce mistaken assumptions

Learning should improve calibration while preserving uncertainty and revision.

## Separation of Intensity and Strategy

Risk determines **how much** support is justified.

Context and purpose determine **how that support should be delivered**.

This separation prevents history or purpose from suppressing a legitimate safety response while still allowing support to remain personally relevant.

Even during Safety Support, the person's purpose remains visible. Safety may temporarily constrain an action, but it should not redefine or erase the person's underlying purpose.
