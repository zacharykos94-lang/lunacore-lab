# LunaCore v0.3 — Physical Foundation Checkpoint

This document defines the stability boundary for the v0.3 general physical foundation.

The goal of v0.3 is not to solve robotics, fluid mechanics, locomotion, embodiment, or hardware design. The goal is to give LunaCore a coherent, testable way to reason about physical sensing and physical change without prematurely choosing how a future embodiment must work.

## Foundation guarantees

The v0.3 physical foundation preserves these separations:

- sensing is separate from state estimation
- state estimation is separate from world modeling
- world knowledge is separate from capability
- desired effect is separate from available capability
- capability is separate from subsystem health
- physical recommendation is separate from authorization
- authorization is separate from physical feasibility
- physical feasibility is separate from available energy
- energy availability is separate from permission to consume it
- abstract output intent is separate from a device command
- readiness summarizes upstream gates but does not override them

## Stable invariants

The following are architectural invariants for this checkpoint:

1. **Unknown remains unknown.** Missing evidence is not silently converted into true, false, reachable, safe, available, or impossible.
2. **Sensor disagreement remains visible.** Materially conflicting physical measurements prefer observation/review rather than forcing an output.
3. **History and trend are revisable.** A measured change rate does not become an automatic prediction of future state.
4. **Equilibrium matters.** Stabilization may take priority over optional movement or interaction.
5. **Permission is not capability.** Human authorization cannot create a missing capability, spatial path, healthy subsystem, physical resource, or physical constraint margin.
6. **Capability is not desire.** Wanting an effect does not imply the embodiment can produce it.
7. **Failure is not hidden.** Required degraded, unavailable, or unknown subsystems are not silently bypassed.
8. **Known hard constraints remain authoritative.** Authorization cannot override a represented hard physical constraint.
9. **Resource stewardship remains upstream of optional physical activity.** Secure shelter, essential loads, conservation state, and reserve policy constrain optional physical energy use.
10. **No embodiment is assumed.** The reasoning core does not select legs, wheels, propellers, pumps, rigid bodies, fluid control, fields, deformation, or another mechanism.
11. **No direct device command is produced by the foundation.** Output remains an abstract intended effect.
12. **Real-world effects remain authorized.** Physical reasoning never grants itself new authority.
13. **Change is closed-loop.** Physical state changes are followed by re-observation rather than assuming the intended result occurred.

## What remains intentionally open

The following are not fixed by v0.3:

- body plan
- number, type, or placement of sensors
- actuator type
- locomotion mechanism
- manipulation mechanism
- geometry or coordinate system
- map representation
- fluid, rigid-body, field, or deformable-medium dynamics
- control algorithm below the bounded abstract feedback layer
- units for physical variables or energy
- domain-specific safety thresholds
- exact equilibrium definition
- communication bus or hardware protocol
- simulation engine
- real-time scheduler
- operating system
- deployment hardware

A later implementation may specialize any of these while preserving the v0.3 interfaces and invariants.

## Medium neutrality

A medium such as `water` is represented as contextual information, not as a commitment to a water-specific machine.

A future implementation could interact with water through flow, buoyancy, pressure, deformation, propulsion, distributed actuation, or another method without requiring the LunaCore reasoning foundation to be rewritten around that method.

The same rule applies to air, ground contact, mixed media, engineered environments, or media not anticipated today.

## Promotion criteria

The physical foundation should be promoted from experimental status only after:

- the full test and strict TypeScript suite remains green
- the invariant conformance suite remains green
- no known upstream gate can disagree with the final abstract-output decision in a way that permits unintended physical action
- architecture review confirms that no mechanism-specific assumption has leaked into the general reasoning layer
- human authorization remains independent and revocable where delegation is allowed
- physical constraints and resource policies remain domain-supplied rather than invented
- future specialization can be added through adapters or lower layers without rewriting the general reasoning model

## Change policy after this checkpoint

Do not expand the physical foundation merely to make it more detailed.

Change the architecture when one of the following provides evidence that the current abstraction is insufficient:

- a failing or missing test exposes a real invariant gap
- an implementation cannot express a necessary physical relationship without violating the current abstraction
- a future embodiment requires a general concept that cannot be represented through existing open interfaces
- empirical testing exposes unsafe or contradictory behavior
- the human governing requirements explicitly change

Mechanism-specific experimentation should normally occur below or beside this foundation, not by making the foundation itself mechanism-specific.

## Checkpoint rule

> Keep the foundation general, inspectable, revisable, resource-aware, authorization-bounded, and closed-loop. Specialize only when evidence requires specialization.
